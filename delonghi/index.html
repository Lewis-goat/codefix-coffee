De'Longhi error code questions

How many De'Longhi error codes are covered?

9 codes across 1 machine family. Each has its own page with the meaning, the part at fault, the fix and the cost.

What does De'Longhi General Alarm (code 1101 / 1512) mean?

Catch-all alarm: the board detected a fault it cannot isolate, most often the infuser (brew unit) or its sensor. General Alarm is De'Longhi's "something is wrong" message. On Magnifica, Dinamica and PrimaDonna machines it is the infuser nine times out of ten: it is stuck, dirty, out of position, or coffee dust is on the optical sensor that reads its position. Scale that makes the pump labour is the next cause.

Can I fix De'Longhi errors myself?

By our ratings, 8 of the 9 codes are easy, 1 moderate and 0 hard or not recommended for DIY. Each page lists the cheapest checks first and says where to stop.
