Ground Too Fine, Adjust MillDe'Longhi

De'Longhi Magnifica / Dinamica Ground Too Fine, Adjust Mill: brew pressure too high

Ground Too Fine, Adjust Mill means: Brew pressure too high: the grind is too fine, or the circuit is blocked.

Applies to: Magnifica, Dinamica and PrimaDonna.

Short answerThe pump hit its pressure limit before the shot finished. On a machine that was working, the grind has not changed; scale in the heater or infuser, or a blocked infuser outlet, is the real cause. It often pairs with "Insert Water Spout" because the machine tries to relieve pressure through the spout.
Part at faultgrinder setting / infuser outlet / water circuit
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix Ground Too Fine, Adjust Mill

  1. Turn the grind dial one or two steps coarser, only while the grinder is running, and try again.
  2. Remove and rinse the infuser; check its outlet is clear.
  3. Run a descale cycle; this is the fix for most "ground too fine" messages on machines older than a year.
  4. Fit the hot-water spout and run water through to confirm the circuit is clear.

When to stop and call a repairer

If hot water will not flow either, the heater or a tube inside is blocked and needs opening up.

Ground Too Fine, Adjust Mill during startup or during use?

Can I keep using the machine with Ground Too Fine, Adjust Mill showing?

Generally yes — Ground Too Fine, Adjust Mill is more of an annoyance than an emergency. Fix it when convenient; just do not let the cause sit for months.

Will Ground Too Fine, Adjust Mill clear on its own?

Often, yes. The first step (turn the grind dial one or two steps coarser, only while the grinder is running, and try again) clears a share of cases on its own. If the code comes straight back, the part is genuinely at fault.
