General Alarm (code 1101 / 1512)De'Longhi

De'Longhi Magnifica / Dinamica General Alarm (code 1101 / 1512): general alarm

General Alarm (code 1101 / 1512) means: Catch-all alarm: the board detected a fault it cannot isolate, most often the infuser (brew unit) or its sensor.

Applies to: Magnifica, Dinamica and PrimaDonna.

Short answerGeneral Alarm is De'Longhi's "something is wrong" message. On Magnifica, Dinamica and PrimaDonna machines it is the infuser nine times out of ten: it is stuck, dirty, out of position, or coffee dust is on the optical sensor that reads its position. Scale that makes the pump labour is the next cause.
Part at faultinfuser assembly and position sensor
Severitymedium

How to fix General Alarm (code 1101 / 1512)

  1. Switch off at the switch on the back, wait 30 seconds, switch on. A one-off clears.
  2. Open the service door, press the two red buttons and pull out the infuser. Rinse it under running water (no soap), work the piston by hand, and let it dry.
  3. Look into the cavity: the small sensor window should be clean; wipe coffee dust off it with a dry cloth.
  4. Refit the infuser until it clicks, with its lever fully down. Close the door and restart.
  5. If the alarm persists, run a descale cycle; a scaled circuit can trigger it.

When to stop and call a repairer

If the infuser will not move by hand or has a cracked seal, replace it (kit 7313251451 or 7313251441 by model).

When does General Alarm (code 1101 / 1512) typically show up?

Can I keep using the machine with General Alarm (code 1101 / 1512) showing?

Usually you can finish the drink you are making, but do not ignore it — the underlying fault rarely heals itself. Work through the cheap checks first.

Will General Alarm (code 1101 / 1512) clear on its own?

Often, yes. The first step (switch off at the switch on the back, wait 30 seconds, switch on. a one-off clears) clears a share of cases on its own. If the code comes straight back, the part is genuinely at fault.

How long does the repair take?

Fifteen to thirty minutes with basic tools, once you have the part.
