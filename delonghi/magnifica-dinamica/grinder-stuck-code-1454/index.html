De'Longhi Magnifica / Dinamica Grinder stuck (code 1454): grinder jammed

Grinder stuck (code 1454) means: Grinder motor stalled.

Short answer. A stone, a very oily bean layer, or grounds packed under the burr have stopped the grinder. Newer models log it as 1454.
Part at faultgrinder burrs and motor
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix Grinder stuck (code 1454)

  1. Switch off, empty the hopper, and vacuum the grinder throat.
  2. Turn the grind dial to the coarsest setting (with the grinder off is fine for this) and back.
  3. Switch on and run a grind with a few beans. Do not force the dial.
  4. If the motor hums without turning, the burr is jammed under the top burr; a service removes and clears it.

When to stop and call a repairer

Do not run the grinder repeatedly against a jam; the motor overheats.

What it costs

Parts: Nothing; grinder assembly $60 to $100.

Service: Out-of-warranty manufacturer service for a super-automatic is typically $250 to $500 including return shipping; independent espresso repairers are usually cheaper for a single part.

Worth repairing? Yes.
