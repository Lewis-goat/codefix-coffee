What does Philips Error 05 mean?

Air is trapped in the water circuit. The pump is running but drawing air rather than water, so no pressure builds. A freshly fitted AquaClean filter that was not primed is the usual cause; an empty tank, a tank not pushed onto its valve, or scale in the inlet are the others.
