Philips / Saeco Error 04: brew group not locked

Error 04 means: Brew group is not correctly placed.

Short answerThe brew group is in the machine but not in its rest position, so the drive cannot engage it. It usually follows a cleaning where the group was refitted with its arm in the wrong place.
Part at faultbrew group position
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix Error 04

  1. Remove the brew group. Check the lever on the side is in the rest position and the two reference marks line up (yellow arrow to arrow on most models).
  2. If it is not, press the top of the group until it clicks down; do not force the lever.
  3. Refit along the guides until it clicks, close the door.
  4. Switch on; the machine will index the group itself.

When to stop and call a repairer

Never force the group in; a cracked group is a new group.

What it costs

PartsNothing; brew group $40 to $80 if damaged.

Generally yes — Error 04 is more of an annoyance than an emergency. Fix it when convenient; just do not let the cause sit for months.
