Philips / Saeco Error 01: grinder outlet blocked

Error 01 means: Coffee grinder cannot work properly: the coffee funnel is blocked with ground coffee.

Short answer. Ground coffee has compacted in the funnel between the grinder and the brew group, usually from oily beans or a very fine grind. The grinder motor stalls against it.
Part at faultcoffee funnel and grinder outlet
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix Error 01

  1. Switch off and wait 20 seconds until the machine is silent.
  2. Remove the brew group. Push the compacted coffee out of the funnel with a spoon handle, then vacuum the loose grounds from the funnel and the brew-group cavity.
  3. Refit the brew group and switch on.
  4. Set the grind one step coarser if it recurs, and avoid very oily dark roasts.

When to stop and call a repairer

If the grinder hums but does not turn with a clear funnel, the grinder is jammed or its motor has failed.

What it costs

Parts: Nothing for a blockage; grinder assembly $60 to $120.

Service: Out-of-warranty manufacturer service for a super-automatic is typically $250 to $500 including return shipping; independent espresso repairers are usually cheaper for a single part.

Worth repairing? Yes.
