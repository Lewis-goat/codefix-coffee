Philips / Saeco Error 03: brew group dirty

Error 03 means: Brew group is too dirty to work properly.

Short answer. The brew group has enough coffee residue on it that it cannot complete its movement. Philips says rinse it; the durable fix is rinse plus lubricate.
Part at faultbrew group
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix Error 03

  1. Switch off, open the service door and remove the brew group.
  2. Rinse it thoroughly under lukewarm water (no soap), work the mechanism by hand, and let it air dry.
  3. Put a little Philips or food-safe silicone grease on the guide rails and the piston seal.
  4. Refit until it clicks; run a brew-group cleaning tablet cycle monthly.

When to stop and call a repairer

Nothing dangerous here.

What it costs

Parts: Grease $8 to $12; brew group $40 to $80.

Service: Out-of-warranty manufacturer service for a super-automatic is typically $250 to $500 including return shipping; independent espresso repairers are usually cheaper for a single part.

Worth repairing? Yes.
