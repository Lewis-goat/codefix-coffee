Breville / Sage Barista Touch ER15: internal communication fault

ER15 means: Communication fault detected between internal electronic modules.

Same code, same fix on the Barista Touch Impress. Everything below applies to both.

Applies to: Barista Touch.

Short answerThe display or main board has lost contact with another board inside the machine. Nine times out of ten that is a ribbon cable that has worked loose, moisture on a connector, or a board that has been splashed by a leak. A genuinely dead board is the rarer, dearer case. ER15 on the Barista Touch is not a sensor code: it is the display board losing communication with the main board. Owners often see it after a leak, after moving the machine, or with a display that flickers first.

How to fix ER15

  1. Unplug for ten minutes, then power back on. Board resets clear a surprising number of these.
  2. Look for a leak. Pull the drip tray, tank and hopper and look for water trails under the machine or on the base plate.
  3. Open the machine and reseat every ribbon cable and plug between the display board, main board and power (triac) board. Push each connector home until it clicks.
  4. Dry any wet connector with a hair dryer on low, and fix the leak that wet it.
  5. If the fault persists with everything dry and seated, one of the boards has failed. Get a quote before ordering: board prices vary a lot by model.

Reseat the ribbon cable between the touch screen and the main board first; it is the most common cause and costs nothing.

If the machine has been leaking from the tank seal or the ThermoJet, the main board may be wet. Dry it and fix the leak before anything else.

When to stop and call a repairer

If the display is blank or flickering as well, do not keep power-cycling; that can take a struggling board from intermittent to dead.

What it costs

PartsNothing if it is a cable; a control board is $100 to $250.
ServiceOut-of-warranty Breville service quotes for internal faults are commonly $300 to $500, sometimes more, plus shipping. Independent espresso repair shops are usually cheaper for a sensor swap.
Worth repairing?Cable or moisture: yes, free. Board: compare the quote with a refurbished machine.

How to check the error log

Switch the machine off at the wall. Hold the front Power button, switch the wall power back on and release when the logo appears. Enter the service password 00000, then open Error Counter to see stored faults or Live Debug for live temperatures.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

ER15 during startup or during use?

Breville / Sage Barista Touchs report ER15 any time. There is no reliable pattern to when it appears — treat any occurrence as real.

Can I keep using the machine with ER15 showing?

Treat it as a stop-using signal: ER15 marks a fault that can damage the machine (or worse) if you keep running it. Do the safe first steps below, then stop.

Will ER15 clear on its own?

Sometimes. Try the reset and cleaning steps first — they cost nothing and clear the milder cases. A code that returns within a day or two needs the parts fix.

How long does the repair take?

Thirty minutes to an hour if you are comfortable opening the case; budget an afternoon the first time.
