Breville / Sage Oracle Jet E16: steam wand blocked

E16 means: Steam wand blocked or abnormal pressure detected during milk operation.

Short answer. The machine tried to texture milk and saw abnormal pressure: the steam tip holes are blocked with dried milk or scale, or the wand path is restricted. E16 on the Oracle Jet is a blocked steam wand or abnormal pressure during milk texturing. The Jet monitors steam pressure, so a milk-crusted tip or a scaled wand trips it before you notice weak steam.
Part at faultsteam wand tip and milk path

How to fix E16

  1. Unscrew the steam tip and soak it in hot water with a little descaler; clear each hole with the pin on the cleaning tool.
  2. Run the auto purge or a manual steam purge for 10 seconds with the tip off, then again with it on.
  3. Descale the machine if it has been more than three months.
  4. If steam is still weak with a clean tip, the wand or its valve is scaled internally: replace the wand assembly.

Soak the tip, pin the holes, run the auto purge, descale. Replace the tip if the holes are enlarged or the wand if it is still restricted.

When to stop and call a repairer

Hot steam: purge into the drip tray, not towards your hand.

What it costs

Parts: Steam tip $10; steam wand assembly $60 to $95.

Service: Out-of-warranty Breville service quotes for internal faults are commonly $300 to $500, sometimes more, plus shipping. Independent espresso repair shops are usually cheaper for a sensor swap.

Worth repairing? Yes.

How to check the error log

Open Settings on the touch screen, then About the Machine, then Software version. On that screen tap each bottom corner of the screen several times. Go back to About the Machine and a new Service center mode entry appears. Open it to view the error log.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.
