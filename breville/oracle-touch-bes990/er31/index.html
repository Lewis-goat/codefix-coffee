Breville / Sage Oracle Touch ER31: tamping / dosing motor fault

ER31 means: Tamping motor timed out during operation.

Same code, same fix on the Oracle. Everything below applies to both.

Short answerThe auto tamper or dosing auger did not finish its travel in the time allowed. Dried coffee oils on the tamper head or in the auger, a portafilter not seated in the cradle, or a very fine grind that packs hard are the usual reasons.
Part at faulttamping motor / tamp fan
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearswhile brewing or steaming

How to fix ER31

  1. Remove the portafilter and wipe the tamper head and cradle with a dry cloth; oily buildup makes the tamper stick.
  2. Check the tamp fan and auger are fitted correctly to the drive shaft after any cleaning.
  3. Run the tamp calibration routine from the settings menu if your model has one.
  4. Coarsen the grind slightly and retry.
  5. If the tamper jams mid-stroke every time, the assembly has failed and is replaced as a unit.

When to stop and call a repairer

Keep fingers clear of the tamper cradle while it moves.

What it costs

PartsNothing for a jam; tamp motor assembly about $80 to $130.
ServiceOut-of-warranty Breville service quotes for internal faults are commonly $300 to $500, sometimes more, plus shipping. Independent espresso repair shops are usually cheaper for a sensor swap.
Worth repairing?Yes.

How to check the error log

Switch the machine off at the wall. Hold the front Power button, switch the wall power back on and release when the logo appears. Enter the service password 00000, then open Error Counter (stored faults) or Live Debug (live temperatures and water levels).

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

Questions

What does ER31 mean on the Breville / Sage Oracle Touch?

Tamping motor timed out during operation. The auto tamper or dosing auger did not finish its travel in the time allowed. Dried coffee oils on the tamper head or in the auger, a portafilter not seated in the cradle, or a very fine grind that packs hard are the usual reasons.

Is it worth repairing?

Yes.

ER31 during startup or during use?

Breville / Sage Oracle Touchs report ER31 while brewing or steaming. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with ER31 showing?

Generally yes — ER31 is more of an annoyance than an emergency. Fix it when convenient; just do not let the cause sit for months.

Will ER31 clear on its own?

Often, yes. The first step (remove the portafilter and wipe the tamper head and cradle with a dry cloth; oily buildup makes the tamper stick) clears a share of cases on its own. If the code comes straight back, the part is genuinely at fault.

How long does the repair take?

Fifteen to thirty minutes with basic tools, once you have the part.
