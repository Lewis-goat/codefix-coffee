Breville / Sage Oracle Touch error codes

32 codes. The Oracle Touch has dual stainless boilers plus heated group head; most codes are one of its temperature sensors, a water-level or flow fault, or the grinder.

How to read the stored error log

Switch the machine off at the wall. Hold the front Power button, switch the wall power back on and release when the logo appears. Enter the service password 00000, then open Error Counter (stored faults) or Live Debug (live temperatures and water levels).

Service menus are intended for technicians. Reading the log is harmless; do not change calibration values.
