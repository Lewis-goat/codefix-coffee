Breville / Sage Oracle Touch ER30: grinder motor fault

ER30 means: Grinder motor timed out during operation.

Same code, same fix on the Oracle. Everything below applies to both.

Short answerThe grinder ran longer than it should without finishing the dose. Almost always this is beans, stones or compacted grounds jamming the burrs, or a grind setting so fine that the motor labours. A worn motor is possible after many thousands of cups.
Part at faultgrinder motor
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearswhile brewing or steaming

How to fix ER30

  1. Stop and let the machine sit for 30 minutes if the code mentions overheating.
  2. Remove the hopper, empty it and check for a stone or a very oily, compacted bean layer at the burr throat.
  3. Lift out the top burr and clean both burrs and the chute with the brush. Never wash burrs; moisture clogs them.
  4. Check the tamp fan / impeller under the burr is fitted and not stuck (Oracle models). A missing fan makes the grinder run continuously.
  5. Set the grind two or three numbers coarser, refit everything, and run a short grind with a small dose of beans.
  6. If the motor hums but does not turn with clean burrs, it has failed. It is a replaceable module.

When to stop and call a repairer

A burning smell means stop and let it cool; do not repeatedly retry a jammed motor.

What it costs

PartsNothing for a jam; a grinder motor is $60 to $120.
ServiceOut-of-warranty Breville service quotes for internal faults are commonly $300 to $500, sometimes more, plus shipping. Independent espresso repair shops are usually cheaper for a sensor swap.
Worth repairing?Yes. Jams are free to fix; a motor is a fair price against a new machine.

How to check the error log

Switch the machine off at the wall. Hold the front Power button, switch the wall power back on and release when the logo appears. Enter the service password 00000, then open Error Counter (stored faults) or Live Debug (live temperatures and water levels).

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

Questions

What does ER30 mean on the Breville / Sage Oracle Touch?

Grinder motor timed out during operation. The grinder ran longer than it should without finishing the dose. Almost always this is beans, stones or compacted grounds jamming the burrs, or a grind setting so fine that the motor labours. A worn motor is possible after many thousands of cups.

Can I fix ER30 myself?

DIY difficulty: easy. Stop and let the machine sit for 30 minutes if the code mentions overheating. Remove the hopper, empty it and check for a stone or a very oily, compacted bean layer at the burr throat.

How much does it cost to fix ER30?

Nothing for a jam; a grinder motor is $60 to $120. Out-of-warranty Breville service quotes for internal faults are commonly $300 to $500, sometimes more, plus shipping. Independent espresso repair shops are usually cheaper for a sensor swap.

Is it worth repairing?

Yes. Jams are free to fix; a motor is a fair price against a new machine.

When does ER30 typically show up?

Breville / Sage Oracle Touchs report ER30 while brewing or steaming. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with ER30 showing?

Generally yes — ER30 is more of an annoyance than an emergency. Fix it when convenient; just do not let the cause sit for months.

Will ER30 clear on its own?

Often, yes. The first step (stop and let the machine sit for 30 minutes if the code mentions overheating) clears a share of cases on its own. If the code comes straight back, the part is genuinely at fault.

How long does the repair take?

Fifteen to thirty minutes with basic tools, once you have the part.
