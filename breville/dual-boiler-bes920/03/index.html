Breville / Sage Dual Boiler 03: temperature sensor short circuit

03 means: Coffee boiler temperature sensor short circuit.

Applies to: Dual Boiler.

Short answerThe coffee boiler temperature sensor (NTC) is reading as a short circuit: the board sees near-zero resistance, which a healthy sensor never produces. The two real-world causes are water bridging the sensor contacts (a leak or steam at the probe seal) and a failed sensor. Because it failed at startup, it is usually a wet connector or a sensor that has already died.

How to fix 03

  1. Switch off at the wall and unplug. Leave the machine for an hour so any moisture in the connectors dries, then try again. A short that clears after drying tells you there is a leak to find.
  2. Open the machine (base or rear panel) and inspect the coffee boiler temperature sensor (NTC) plug and the wiring near the boiler. Look for a wet patch, white scale trails or a perished o-ring around the probe.
  3. Replace any leaking o-rings at the boiler fittings and NTC probes. Kits are cheap and the leak, not the sensor, is often the true fault.
  4. If the code persists after everything is dry, replace the coffee boiler temperature sensor (NTC).
  5. Clear the error log and run the machine through a full heat-up and a shot to confirm.

When to stop and call a repairer

If you see scorch marks on the triac board or the machine trips your GFCI / RCD, stop: that is an electrical fault, not just a sensor.

What it costs

PartsGenuine NTC sensor assemblies run about $25 to $95 depending on which sensor it is (steam wand and milk-jug sensors are the dear ones). O-ring kits are $10 to $20.
ServiceOut-of-warranty Breville service quotes for internal faults are commonly $300 to $500, sometimes more, plus shipping. Independent espresso repair shops are usually cheaper for a sensor swap.
Worth repairing?Yes in most cases. Sensor plus o-ring kit is well under $100. If a leak has soaked the control board, get a quote before spending anything.

How to check the error log

Switch the machine off at the wall. Hold EXIT and MANUAL while switching the power back on to enter the self-check menu. Press MENU to reach item 3 (error log) and item 4 (boiler level status such as LLL / HHH). In the error log, MENU steps through codes 00 to 12 with a count for each. At "ErSt", hold MANUAL until it beeps to clear the codes (the cup counter is not reset).

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

03 during startup or during use?

Breville / Sage Dual Boilers report 03 any time. There is no reliable pattern to when it appears — treat any occurrence as real.

Can I keep using the machine with 03 showing?

Usually you can finish the drink you are making, but do not ignore it — the underlying fault rarely heals itself. Work through the cheap checks first.

Will 03 clear on its own?

Sometimes. Try the reset and cleaning steps first — they cost nothing and clear the milder cases. A code that returns within a day or two needs the parts fix.

Thirty minutes to an hour if you are comfortable opening the case; budget an afternoon the first time.
