Breville / Sage Oracle Error 11: temperature sensor short circuit

Error 11 means: Group head temperature sensor short circuit at startup.

The Breville / Sage Oracle shows this code with identical meaning and fix as the Oracle Touch. Full guide for ER11.

Short answerThe group head temperature sensor (NTC) is reading as a short circuit: the board sees near-zero resistance, which a healthy sensor never produces. The two real-world causes are water bridging the sensor contacts (a leak or steam at the probe seal) and a failed sensor. Because it failed at startup, it is usually a wet connector or a sensor that has already died.
Part at faultgroup head temperature sensor (NTC)

How to fix Error 11

  1. Switch off at the wall and unplug. Leave the machine for an hour so any moisture in the connectors dries, then try again. A short that clears after drying tells you there is a leak to find.
  2. Open the machine (base or rear panel) and inspect the group head temperature sensor (NTC) plug and the wiring near the boiler. Look for a wet patch, white scale trails or a perished o-ring around the probe.
  3. Replace any leaking o-rings at the boiler fittings and NTC probes. Kits are cheap and the leak, not the sensor, is often the true fault.
  4. If the code persists after everything is dry, replace the group head temperature sensor (NTC).
  5. Clear the error log and run the machine through a full heat-up and a shot to confirm.

When to stop and call a repairer

If you see scorch marks on the triac board or the machine trips your GFCI / RCD, stop: that is an electrical fault, not just a sensor.

What it costs

PartsGenuine NTC sensor assemblies run about $25 to $95 depending on which sensor it is (steam wand and milk-jug sensors are the dear ones). O-ring kits are $10 to $20.
ServiceOut-of-warranty Breville service quotes for internal faults are commonly $300 to $500, sometimes more, plus shipping. Independent espresso repair shops are usually cheaper for a sensor swap.
Worth repairing?Yes in most cases. Sensor plus o-ring kit is well under $100. If a leak has soaked the control board, get a quote before spending anything.

How to check the error log

Leave the machine plugged in but switched off. Press and hold 1 CUP, 2 CUP and POWER together for at least one second. After the long beep the LCD shows the firmware version (starts with "Fr"). Press the SELECT dial once to open Error Storage, then rotate it to step through errors 1 to 32 and the stored count for each. Scroll past error 32 to "ErSt" and hold SELECT for 3 seconds to clear the log.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

Error 11 during startup or during use?

Breville / Sage Oracles report Error 11 during the power-on self-test. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with Error 11 showing?

Usually you can finish the drink you are making, but do not ignore it — the underlying fault rarely heals itself. Work through the cheap checks first.

Will Error 11 clear on its own?

Sometimes. Try the reset and cleaning steps first — they cost nothing and clear the milder cases. A code that returns within a day or two needs the parts fix.
