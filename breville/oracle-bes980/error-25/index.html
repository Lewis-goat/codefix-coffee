Breville / Sage Oracle Error 25: overheated above target temperature

Error 25 means: Coffee boiler overheated above target temperature.

The Breville / Sage Oracle shows this code with identical meaning and fix as the Oracle Touch. Full guide for ER25.

Short answer. The coffee boiler and pump got hotter than the controller asked for. That means either the heater kept running when it was told to stop (a triac on the power board latched on) or the sensor is under-reading because of a leak or steam at the probe seal. Breville machines cut power to protect themselves, and repeated overheating blows the thermal fuse.

How to fix Error 25

  1. Switch off at the wall immediately and let the machine cool for at least 60 minutes.
  2. Switch back on and watch the heat-up. If it reaches temperature normally and the code does not return, treat it as a one-off caused by low water or a blocked flow and descale.
  3. Check the boiler fittings and NTC probe for leaks and replace perished o-rings.
  4. If the machine overheats again or steam pours from the group head at idle, the triac board is latched on. This is a board replacement, not a cleaning job.
  5. If the machine now will not heat at all after an overheat, the thermal fuse has done its job and blown. It is a cheap part but sits on the boiler and needs the machine opened up.

When to stop and call a repairer

Do not keep running a machine that repeats an overheat code. Latched heaters can boil dry and damage the boiler and wiring.

What it costs

Parts: Thermal fuse $10 to $20; triac board $80 to $150; steam boiler probe and o-ring kit about $85.

Service: Out-of-warranty Breville service quotes for internal faults are commonly $300 to $500, sometimes more, plus shipping. Independent espresso repair shops are usually cheaper for a sensor swap.

Worth repairing? Depends on the cause. Probe o-rings: yes, always. Triac board plus thermal fuse on a machine over five years old: get the part price first and compare with a refurbished unit.

How to check the error log

Leave the machine plugged in but switched off. Press and hold 1 CUP, 2 CUP and POWER together for at least one second. After the long beep the LCD shows the firmware version (starts with "Fr"). Press the SELECT dial once to open Error Storage, then rotate it to step through errors 1 to 32 and the stored count for each. Scroll past error 32 to "ErSt" and hold SELECT for 3 seconds to clear the log.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

Error 25 during startup or during use?

Breville / Sage Oracles report Error 25 any time. There is no reliable pattern to when it appears — treat any occurrence as real.

Can I keep using the machine with Error 25 showing?

Treat it as a stop-using signal: Error 25 marks a fault that can damage the machine (or worse) if you keep running it. Do the safe first steps below, then stop.

Will Error 25 clear on its own?

Rarely. A reset or power cycle is worth one try, but Error 25 with a "hard" difficulty rating almost always means a failed component that has to be replaced.
