GE Appliances dryer E7: power supply fault

E7 means: Electrical connection failure (cord, outlet or breaker).

Short answerThe dryer is not seeing both legs of its 240 V supply. A tripped half of a double breaker, a loose terminal block connection, or a damaged cord.
Part at faultpower supply
Severitymedium
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix E7

  1. Check the double breaker: reset it fully off then on.
  2. Unplug and inspect the cord and the terminal block at the back for a loose or burnt connection.
  3. Test the outlet for 240 V between the two hot legs.

When to stop and call a repairer

The terminal block carries 240 V; unplug before touching it.

What it costs

PartsCord $15 to $30; terminal block $10 to $20.
ServiceA home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.
Worth repairing?Yes.

How to check the error log

On newer GE dryers (GTD/GFD 2016 on), with the dryer off, press and hold the Signal and Temp buttons for 5 seconds to enter service mode; the last stored error shows. On SmartHQ models the app lists stored codes. Unplugging for 5 minutes resets the board.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

Questions

What does E7 mean on the GE Appliances dryer?

Can I fix E7 myself?

DIY difficulty: easy. Check the double breaker: reset it fully off then on. Unplug and inspect the cord and the terminal block at the back for a loose or burnt connection.

How much does it cost to fix E7?

Is it worth repairing?

Yes.

E7 during startup or during use?

GE Appliances dryers report E7 any. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with E7 showing?

Usually you can finish the drink you are making, but do not ignore it — the underlying fault rarely heals itself. Work through the cheap checks first.

Will E7 clear on its own?

Often, yes. The first step (check the double breaker: reset it fully off then on) clears a share of cases on its own. If the code comes straight back, the part is genuinely at fault.

How long does the repair take?

Fifteen to thirty minutes with basic tools, once you have the part.
