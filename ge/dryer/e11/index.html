GE Appliances dryer E11: drive motor fault

E11 means: Drum motor problem: motor not turning properly or a motor switch needs replacing.

Short answerThe motor is not doing what the board asks. A worn belt, a seized drum roller, or the motor start switch or motor itself.
Part at faultdrive motor
Severitymedium
DIY difficultyhard
When it appearsany

How to fix E11

  1. Unplug and turn the drum by hand; a stiff drum is rollers or bearing, not the motor.
  2. Open the cabinet and check the belt and idler.
  3. Replace the motor if it hums and does not start with the belt off.

When to stop and call a repairer

Unplug before opening the cabinet.

What it costs

PartsBelt $15 to $25; rollers $20 to $40; motor $80 to $150.
ServiceA home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.
Worth repairing?Depends on which part.

How to check the error log

On newer GE dryers (GTD/GFD 2016 on), with the dryer off, press and hold the Signal and Temp buttons for 5 seconds to enter service mode; the last stored error shows. On SmartHQ models the app lists stored codes. Unplugging for 5 minutes resets the board.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

E11 during startup or during use?

GE Appliances dryers report E11 any. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with E11 showing?

Usually you can finish the drink you are making, but do not ignore it — the underlying fault rarely heals itself. Work through the cheap checks first.

Will E11 clear on its own?

Rarely. A reset or power cycle is worth one try, but E11 with a "hard" difficulty rating almost always means a failed component that has to be replaced.

How long does the repair take?

This one is a bench repair for most owners — the teardown, test and reassembly are the bulk of the work, not the part itself.
