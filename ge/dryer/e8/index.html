GE Appliances dryer E8: motor speed sensor error

E8 means: Motor tachometer (speed sensor) error: no speed signal from the drive motor (older models: drum light or drain fault).

Short answer. On current GE dryers E8 means the board switched the motor on and never got the speed signal back. Loose tach wiring on the motor, a jammed drum (belt off, something behind the drum), or a failed tach or motor. On some older or combo models E8 is a different fault, so check your model.

How to fix E8

  1. Unplug for 5 minutes and restart.
  2. Turn the drum by hand: if it is stiff or jammed, the belt or an obstruction is stalling the motor.
  3. Unplug, open the cabinet, and reseat the tach connector on the motor; check the harness for chafing.
  4. If the motor runs but the code persists, replace the motor (the tach is part of it).

When to stop and call a repairer

Unplug before opening the cabinet.

What it costs

Parts: Belt $15 to $25; drive motor $80 to $150.

Service: A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Worth repairing? Belt or connector: yes. Motor on an old dryer: judgment call.

How to check the error log

On newer GE dryers (GTD/GFD 2016 on), with the dryer off, press and hold the Signal and Temp buttons for 5 seconds to enter service mode; the last stored error shows. On SmartHQ models the app lists stored codes. Unplugging for 5 minutes resets the board.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.
