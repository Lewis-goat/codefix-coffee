GE Appliances dryer E14: stuck key

E14 means: Stuck key on the control panel.

Applies to: dryer.

Short answerA button is being read as held down. Dirt or moisture around a key, or a failed membrane.
Part at faultcontrol panel
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix E14

  1. Press every button once to free a stuck one; wipe the panel with a barely damp cloth.
  2. Unplug for 30 seconds and restart.
  3. Replace the control panel if it persists.

When to stop and call a repairer

Nothing dangerous here.

What it costs

PartsControl panel $60 to $150.
ServiceA home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.
Worth repairing?Yes.

How to check the error log

GE does not publish a consumer diagnostic-mode sequence for its dryers. Field-service mode and its entry keys differ by platform and are in the technical service manual for your model number. Sequences posted online are for single models and some do not work, so we do not list them. Unplugging for five minutes clears a transient fault.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

Questions

What does E14 mean on the GE Appliances dryer?

Stuck key on the control panel. A button is being read as held down. Dirt or moisture around a key, or a failed membrane.

Can I fix E14 myself?

DIY difficulty: easy. Press every button once to free a stuck one; wipe the panel with a barely damp cloth. Unplug for 30 seconds and restart.

How much does it cost to fix E14?

Control panel $60 to $150. A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Is it worth repairing?

Yes.

E14 during startup or during use?

GE Appliances dryers report E14 any. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with E14 showing?

Generally yes — E14 is more of an annoyance than an emergency. Fix it when convenient; just do not let the cause sit for months.

Will E14 clear on its own?

Often, yes. The first step (press every button once to free a stuck one; wipe the panel with a barely damp cloth) clears a share of cases on its own. If the code comes straight back, the part is genuinely at fault.

How long does the repair take?

Fifteen to thirty minutes with basic tools, once you have the part.
