GE Appliances dishwasher C2: slow drain (pump-out over 7 minutes)

C2 means: Total pump-out time exceeded 7 minutes (GE definition): the dishwasher is draining too slowly.

Applies to: dishwasher.

Short answerGE defines C2 as the total pump-out time going over seven minutes, which means water is leaving far too slowly or not at all. A partly blocked filter or drain hose, a drain pump that is weak or jammed, or a blocked line.
Part at faultdrain pump motor / drain line
Severitymedium
DIY difficultymoderate
When it appearsany

How to fix C2

  1. Start/Reset, wait two minutes, then switch the dishwasher off at the breaker for about 30 seconds and restore it (GE's own reset guidance).
  2. Clean the filter and sump and clear the drain hose; check the air gap if fitted.
  3. Listen for the drain pump at the start of a drain. If it is silent or only hums, check its plug and wiring.
  4. Replace the drain pump if it is dead or jammed.

When to stop and call a repairer

Disconnect power before reaching into the sump.

What it costs

PartsDrain pump $40 to $80.
ServiceA home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.
Worth repairing?Yes.

How to check the error log

GE does not publish a consumer diagnostic-mode sequence for its dishwashers. Service-mode entry differs by model and lives in the technical service manual for your model number (printed on the tub or door edge). Button sequences circulating online are model-specific and often wrong, and service mode can run pumps, valves and heaters, so we do not list them. GE's own reset is: Start/Reset, wait two minutes, then switch off at the breaker for about 30 seconds.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.
