GE Appliances dishwasher C6: water not heating

C6 means: Water temperature too low after the extended heating period.

Applies to: dishwasher.

Short answerThe water never reached about 120 F. If your incoming hot water is cold when the cycle starts the heater may not catch up; otherwise the heating element or its thermostat has failed.
Part at faultheating element, thermostat and hot-water supply
Severitymedium
DIY difficultymoderate
When it appearsany

How to fix C6

  1. Run the kitchen tap until it is hot before starting the dishwasher; set the water heater to 120 F.
  2. If the dishes are cold and wet at the end of a cycle, the element is not heating.
  3. Meter the heating element for continuity; replace it (or the high-limit thermostat) if open.

When to stop and call a repairer

Disconnect power before touching the element wiring.

What it costs

PartsHeating element $30 to $60; thermostat $10 to $20.
ServiceA home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.
Worth repairing?Yes.

How to check the error log

GE does not publish a consumer diagnostic-mode sequence for its dishwashers. Service-mode entry differs by model and lives in the technical service manual for your model number (printed on the tub or door edge). Button sequences circulating online are model-specific and often wrong, and service mode can run pumps, valves and heaters, so we do not list them. GE's own reset is: Start/Reset, wait two minutes, then switch off at the breaker for about 30 seconds.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

C6 during startup or during use?

Can I keep using the machine with C6 showing?

Usually you can finish the drink you are making, but do not ignore it — the underlying fault rarely heals itself. Work through the cheap checks first.

Will C6 clear on its own?

Sometimes. Try the reset and cleaning steps first — they cost nothing and clear the milder cases. A code that returns within a day or two needs the parts fix.

How long does the repair take?

Thirty minutes to an hour if you are comfortable opening the case; budget an afternoon the first time.
