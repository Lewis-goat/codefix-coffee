GE Appliances dishwasher C6: water not heating

C6 means: Water temperature too low after the extended heating period.

Short answer. The water never reached about 120 F. If your incoming hot water is cold when the cycle starts the heater may not catch up; otherwise the heating element or its thermostat has failed.
Part at faultheating element, thermostat and hot-water supply
Severitymedium
DIY difficultymoderate
When it appearsany

How to fix C6

  1. Run the kitchen tap until it is hot before starting the dishwasher; set the water heater to 120 F.
  2. If the dishes are cold and wet at the end of a cycle, the element is not heating.
  3. Meter the heating element for continuity; replace it (or the high-limit thermostat) if open.

When to stop and call a repairer

Disconnect power before touching the element wiring.

What it costs

Parts: Heating element $30 to $60; thermostat $10 to $20.

Service: A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Worth repairing? Yes.

How to check the error log

On most GE dishwashers, hold Start for 5 seconds with the door open to enter the service menu; on models with a hidden display, press Select Cycle and Start together for 5 seconds. The last error shows on the display. Turn power off at the breaker for 60 seconds to reset the board.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.
