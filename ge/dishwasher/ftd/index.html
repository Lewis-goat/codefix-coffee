GE Appliances dishwasher FTD: failed to drain

FTD means: Failed to drain.

Short answerSame as C1 to C3 on newer models: the pump ran and the water stayed.
Part at faultdrain pump and hose
Severitymedium
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix FTD

  1. Clean the filter and sump.
  2. Clear the drain hose and check the disposal knockout.
  3. Reset at the breaker and run a drain.
  4. Replace the drain pump if it is silent or hums.

When to stop and call a repairer

Disconnect power before working in the sump.

What it costs

PartsDrain pump $40 to $80.
ServiceA home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.
Worth repairing?Yes.

How to check the error log

On most GE dishwashers, hold Start for 5 seconds with the door open to enter the service menu; on models with a hidden display, press Select Cycle and Start together for 5 seconds. The last error shows on the display. Turn power off at the breaker for 60 seconds to reset the board.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

Questions

What does FTD mean on the GE Appliances dishwasher?

Failed to drain. Same as C1 to C3 on newer models: the pump ran and the water stayed.

Can I fix FTD myself?

DIY difficulty: easy. Clean the filter and sump. Clear the drain hose and check the disposal knockout.

How much does it cost to fix FTD?

Is it worth repairing?

Yes.

When does FTD typically show up?

GE Appliances dishwashers report FTD any. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with FTD showing?

Usually you can finish the drink you are making, but do not ignore it — the underlying fault rarely heals itself. Work through the cheap checks first.

Will FTD clear on its own?

Often, yes. The first step (clean the filter and sump) clears a share of cases on its own. If the code comes straight back, the part is genuinely at fault.

How long does the repair take?

Fifteen to thirty minutes with basic tools, once you have the part.
