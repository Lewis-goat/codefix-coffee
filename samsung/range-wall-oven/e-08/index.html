E-08Samsung

Samsung range and wall oven E-08: oven not heating

E-08 means: Oven not heating: bake/broil element or temperature sensor fault (on some models: door lock fault).

Applies to: range and wall oven.

Short answerThe board set a temperature and the oven did not climb. The bake element has burnt out (look for a blister or break on it), the temperature sensor is off, or a relay on the board did not close. Some Samsung support pages list E-08 as the door-lock motor; check your model manual.
Part at faultbake element, broil element, oven sensor
Severitymedium
DIY difficultymoderate
When it appearsany

How to fix E-08

  1. Cut power for 3 minutes and restore.
  2. Look at the bake element with the oven on: it should glow evenly. A visible break or blister means replace it.
  3. Meter the oven sensor: about 1080 ohms at room temperature.
  4. If element and sensor are fine, the relay board is not switching the element.

When to stop and call a repairer

Cut power before touching element terminals.

What it costs

PartsBake element $30 to $60; sensor $20 to $40; relay board $100 to $200.
ServiceA home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.
Worth repairing?Element or sensor: yes.

How to check the error log

Samsung ovens do not have a user-accessible error log. The code stays on the display until you fix the cause or cut power at the breaker for 3 minutes; if it returns after a reset, treat it as real.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

How much does it cost to fix E-08?

Is it worth repairing?

Element or sensor: yes.

E-08 during startup or during use?

Samsung range and wall ovens report E-08 any. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with E-08 showing?

Usually you can finish the drink you are making, but do not ignore it — the underlying fault rarely heals itself. Work through the cheap checks first.

Will E-08 clear on its own?

Sometimes. Try the reset and cleaning steps first — they cost nothing and clear the milder cases. A code that returns within a day or two needs the parts fix.

How long does the repair take?

Thirty minutes to an hour if you are comfortable opening the case; budget an afternoon the first time.
