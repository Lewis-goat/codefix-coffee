C-F2Samsung

Samsung range and wall oven C-F2: cooling fan feedback fault

C-F2 means: Cooling-fan / control communication fault — the display board is not receiving the expected feedback from the cooling fan circuit.

Applies to: range and wall oven.

Short answerThe C-F family on Samsung ranges is the control system reporting that a monitored component is not answering — with C-F2 it is the cooling fan circuit. Either the fan is genuinely not running, its connector is loose or scorched, or the feedback line to the board has failed. Unlike C-21/C-24 this is usually an electrical fault rather than a genuine overheat.
Part at faultcooling fan and its feedback circuit
Severitymedium
DIY difficultymoderate
When it appearsany

How to fix C-F2

  1. Breaker reset for 5 minutes, then heat the oven and confirm whether the cooling fan physically spins.
  2. Fan spinning but code persists: the feedback path is the fault — reseat the fan connector on the board and look for heat-discoloured pins.
  3. Fan not spinning: check for a jammed blade (dust, a dropped screw behind the panel), then measure the fan for an open winding.
  4. Replace the fan if it is dead; the code clears with a working feedback signal.
  5. A fan and connector that both check out with C-F2 persisting points to the main board input.

When to stop and call a repairer

Unplug the range before working behind the control panel.

What it costs

PartsCooling fan $40 to $90; connector repair $10; main PCB $150 to $300.
ServiceA home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.
Worth repairing?Yes — fan and connector fixes are cheap.

How to check the error log

Samsung ovens do not have a user-accessible error log. The code stays on the display until you fix the cause or cut power at the breaker for 3 minutes; if it returns after a reset, treat it as real.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

Can I keep using the machine with C-F2 showing?
