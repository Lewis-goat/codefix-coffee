Samsung range and wall oven C-d0: stuck key

C-d0 means: Stuck key detected.

Applies to: range and wall oven.

Short answerSame as SE on many models: a key is being read as pressed.
Part at faultcontrol panel membrane
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix C-d0

  1. Press the key that shows as stuck, and all others once.
  2. Cut power for 3 minutes.
  3. Replace the membrane if it persists.

When to stop and call a repairer

Nothing dangerous here.

What it costs

PartsMembrane $40 to $90.
ServiceA home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.
Worth repairing?Yes.

How to check the error log

Samsung ovens do not have a user-accessible error log. The code stays on the display until you fix the cause or cut power at the breaker for 3 minutes; if it returns after a reset, treat it as real.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

Questions

What does C-d0 mean on the Samsung range and wall oven?

Stuck key detected. Same as SE on many models: a key is being read as pressed.

Can I fix C-d0 myself?

DIY difficulty: easy. Press the key that shows as stuck, and all others once. Cut power for 3 minutes.

How much does it cost to fix C-d0?

Membrane $40 to $90. A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Is it worth repairing?

Yes.

C-d0 during startup or during use?

Samsung range and wall ovens report C-d0 any. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with C-d0 showing?

Generally yes — C-d0 is more of an annoyance than an emergency. Fix it when convenient; just do not let the cause sit for months.

Will C-d0 clear on its own?

Often, yes. The first step (press the key that shows as stuck, and all others once) clears a share of cases on its own. If the code comes straight back, the part is genuinely at fault.

How long does the repair take?

Fifteen to thirty minutes with basic tools, once you have the part.
