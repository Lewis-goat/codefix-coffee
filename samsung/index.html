Samsung error code questions

How many Samsung error codes are covered?

18 codes across 1 machine family. Each has its own page with the meaning, the part at fault, the fix and the cost.

What does Samsung SE mean?

Defective or stuck key on the control panel (short in the touchpad membrane). The board sees a key held down or shorted. Moisture or grease under the membrane, a stuck button, or a failed membrane. On older ranges it can also be the ribbon cable to the control board.

What does Samsung LE mean?

Low voltage fault (board supply under about 9 V DC). The board is not getting its low-voltage supply. A loose connector or a failing transformer on the board.

Can I fix Samsung errors myself?

By our ratings, 1 of the 18 codes are easy, 4 moderate and 13 hard or not recommended for DIY. Each page lists the cheapest checks first and says where to stop.
