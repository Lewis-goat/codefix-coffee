Gaggia troubleshooting: lights, display messages and descaling

Gaggia machines fall into three groups: a few plain lights (Classic Pro and its siblings), a panel of symbols (Brera) and a display with numbered errors (Anima, Cadorna, Magenta). This page sorts them by family and gives the manufacturer's own checks for descaling, no steam, weak flow and leaks.

Models covered: Classic Pro, Classic Evo Pro (and the Classic E24), Brera, Anima, Cadorna Prestige and Magenta Prestige. We read Gaggia's manuals and Gaggia North America's support pages. Where we could not verify something, we say so.

We advise: unplug the machine and let it cool before opening, cleaning or descaling it. Boilers, steam wands and the group head stay hot after use. Do not open a machine that has a power or heating fault. Gaggia's own manuals say there are no user-serviceable parts inside.

The manual has no row for "no pressure" or for a leak from under the machine. For anything not in its table, contact Gaggia or an authorised service centre.

Brera: symbol panel

Gaggia North America lists 18 Brera signals. The page shows them as pictures, so we describe the conditions, not the symbols. The ones people ask about most:

  • Descale needed. Run the descaling cycle. The sheet and the page say to hold the aroma/pre-ground key for 5 seconds to enter it. A later errata gives a different start (see the descaling table).
  • No water. Fill the tank.
  • Service door, knob or brew group out of place. A blinking symbol. If it happens while a drink is being dispensed, the machine stops and counts down 30 seconds before cancelling. Closing the door or knob, or refitting the group, within that time lets it carry on from where it stopped.
  • Refill circuit. Turn the knob to the cup symbol.
  • Empty the dreg drawer. The sheet says to wait for the icon to flash (5 seconds) to reset the dreg counter.

In the Brera troubleshooting sheet, "no hot water or steam" has two listed causes: a clogged hole in the steam wand, or a dirty Pannarello. The sheet says to clean the hole with a pin, with the machine off and cooled. A slow warm-up or low water from the wand points to limescale, and the fix is to descale. Coffee leaking from the dispensing head is attributed to a clogged head that needs cleaning.

Gaggia North America's Cadorna series page lists 12 codes with different wording from the manual, and we only read it through a page summary, so treat this as medium confidence. For the Cadorna Prestige, follow the manual table above. The page lists 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 19 and 20. It describes 02 as a disconnected grinder (check the wiring), 03 and 04 as a brew unit blocked in a position (remove it and trip the microswitch), and 05 as a clogged hydraulic circuit. For 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 19 and 20 it says the unit needs professional repair. Its wording for 14 ("temperature was up to 170") differs from the manual, which says overheated and to wait 30 minutes. We do not interpret 19.

For other Anima codes, the Anima Deluxe manual says to switch off, switch back on after 30 seconds and try 2 or 3 times, then contact the Gaggia hotline with the code. Both the Magenta and Cadorna manuals say to call an authorised service centre if a code stays.

The Magenta and Cadorna manuals tell you to remove the INTENZA+ water filter before descaling. The Anima manual says to swap it for the small white filter. All three say to empty the drip tray and the grounds container first. The Magenta and Cadorna manuals say failing to descale voids the warranty. The Anima manual says repairs are not covered if you ignore the CALC CLEAN symbol.

If a descale is interrupted

The Cadorna, Magenta and Anima manuals give the same recovery, with different exit buttons: ON/OFF (held a few seconds on the Magenta), or STAND-BY on the Anima. Empty and wash the tank and fill it to the CALC CLEAN level with fresh water. The Magenta and Cadorna then say to switch on and let the machine rinse automatically. All three say to run a manual rinse before any drink and to repeat the descale soon, since the cycle is unfinished. The Brera errata says that if the machine is switched off mid-cycle, the cycle must be repeated from the point where it paused.

Questions

Does the Gaggia Classic Pro show error codes?

No. The Classic Pro, Classic Evo Pro and Classic E24 share one operating manual (RI9380). It describes three lights: power, coffee temperature and steam temperature. It has no code table and does not explain flashing patterns. If a light behaves oddly, the manual gives no official meaning, so contact Gaggia or an authorised service centre.

How often should I descale a Gaggia?

It depends on the family. The Classic manual says every 2 months. Its safety notes also say descaling is still recommended every 2 to 3 months if you use purified or bottled water. The Anima, Cadorna and Magenta machines tell you on the display when to descale. A Gaggia errata for the Brera says every 1 to 2 months for very hard water or light use, even without a signal. The Classic manual and the Brera errata say never to use vinegar. The Anima, Cadorna and Magenta manuals say to use only the Gaggia solution, because other products may damage the machine.

My Gaggia has no steam. What should I check?

On the Classic family, press the steam button and wait for the steam light, which the manual puts at 15 to 20 seconds after you press it. The manual then says to open the knob slightly to clear condensation from the wand and close it again. It has no row for a wand that gives no steam; for milk that will not froth it says to check that the nozzle is not clogged. The manual also warns that more than 60 seconds of continuous steam can empty the boiler, after which you reload the circuit. On the Brera, the manual lists a clogged steam wand hole or a dirty Pannarello as causes of no steam or hot water.

Why is there water under my Gaggia?

For the Cadorna Prestige and Magenta Prestige, the manuals say an overfull drip tray that has spilled over is the listed cause. Empty it when the indicator shows through the grid, and before you start a descale. For the Classic family, the manual lists only leaking at the filter holder: check the holder is locked in, the seal is clean and the rim is free of coffee. We found no official guidance on a leak from inside the machine. That needs a technician.

Can I get out of a stuck descaling cycle by holding a button?

The manuals differ. The Magenta Prestige says to press the ON/OFF button for a few seconds. The Cadorna Prestige says to press ON/OFF. The Anima Deluxe says to press STAND-BY. Each then says to empty and wash the tank, fill it to the CALC CLEAN level and run the rinse steps, and to repeat the descale soon. The Magenta and Cadorna manuals also say you can pause and resume with START/STOP. We do not repeat hold-for-10-seconds tips from forums, because we found no Gaggia source for them.

Does this page cover every Anima, Cadorna and Magenta?

No. We read the Anima Deluxe manual, the Cadorna Prestige manual and the Magenta Prestige manual, plus Gaggia North America's series pages. We did not read separate manuals for the Cadorna Barista Plus, Cadorna Milk or Magenta Plus. Check the manual for your exact model.
