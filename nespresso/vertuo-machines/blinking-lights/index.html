Nespresso Vertuo Blinking lights: light-pattern decoder

Blinking lights means: The Vertuo light patterns are the machine error language: blink colour and count identify heating, descale and fault states.

Short answer. Vertuo machines speak in blinks, and Nespresso documents the patterns on its assistance pages. The recurring ones: alternating/sequential blinks during start-up are normal heating; blinking orange is descale-family (needed, in progress, or overdue); a steady or repeating red is a fault state — typically overheating or an internal error — where the first move is unplugging long enough for the machine to cool and reset.

How to fix Blinking lights

  1. Count what the light does before touching anything: colour, steady vs blinking, and how many blinks per group.
  2. Sequential or alternating blinks right after power-on: heating mode — wait 15 to 25 seconds for ready; not a fault.
  3. Orange in any pattern: run the full descale cycle. Blinking orange that never ends usually means a descale that was started and never finished.
  4. Steady or repeating red: unplug for at least 10 minutes (let it fully cool if it was hot), then retry. Red that returns immediately needs support.
  5. Do the 5-press factory reset last, not first — it wipes descale reminders and pairing without fixing mechanical causes.

When to stop and call a repairer

Nothing dangerous; do not descale with vinegar — it damages the circuit and voids help.

What it costs

Parts: $0 to $15 (descaler).
