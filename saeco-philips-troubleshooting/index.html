Saeco and Philips coffee machine troubleshooting

For Xelsis, LatteGo, Incanto and Saeco super-automatics. Find your error code, see whether it is something you can clear yourself, and open its page for the steps.

Codes you can try to clear yourself

CodeMeaningFirst thing to try
Error 01Coffee grinder cannot work properly: the coffee funnel is blocked with ground coffeeSwitch off and wait 20 seconds until the machine is silent.
Error 03Brew group is too dirty to work properlySwitch off, open the service door and remove the brew group.
Error 04Brew group is not correctly placedRemove the brew group. Check the lever on the side is in the rest position and the two reference marks line up (yellow arrow to arrow on most models).
Error 05Air is trapped in the water circuitEmpty and refill the tank; if you have just fitted an AquaClean filter, shake it for five seconds, hold it upside down in a jug of water until no bubbles rise, then refit.
Error 11 or 19Machine needs to adjust to room temperature (too cold after transport)Switch off and leave the machine at room temperature for at least 30 minutes.
Error 14Machine has overheatedSwitch off and leave it for 30 minutes to cool.
Error 20Brew group not detected in position, or a service component (drip tray, grounds container, service door) not seated — the Xelsis-class fault family Philips routes to the brew unit and door switchesSwitch off and unplug. Refit the drip tray and the coffee grounds container firmly, close the service door fully, then switch on again — this is the manual-documented reset for the family.

Questions

Do Saeco, Philips, Xelsis and LatteGo machines share error codes?

They share one numbered scheme, and Philips owns the Saeco brand. Some codes and what they mean differ slightly between ranges, so each code page says what it covers. The page for Error 20, for example, applies to the brew unit and service door on these machines.

What should I do first when a Saeco or Philips coffee machine shows an error?

Check the water tank, drip tray and grounds container are seated, then switch the machine off and on. 7 of the 8 codes we cover are rated easy to clear yourself; the rest usually need a part or a service.

Which Saeco error codes can I fix myself?

The easy ones are Error 01, Error 03, Error 04, Error 05, Error 11 or 19, Error 14, Error 20. The page for each code lists the cheapest checks first and says where to stop.

What does Saeco Xelsis error 20 mean?

Brew group not detected in position, or a service component (drip tray, grounds container, service door) not seated — the Xelsis-class fault family Philips routes to the brew unit and door switches. Open the Error 20 page for the steps.
