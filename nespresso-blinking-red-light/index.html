Nespresso blinking red light: what it means

Red lights are not the same across Nespresso machines. We sorted what Nespresso's own manuals and support pages say by machine family, and we mark the gaps where Nespresso gives no red light meaning.

Before you start: note your model name first. The same pattern can mean different things on different families. Our general Nespresso light meanings page covers Original blink patterns, and the Vertuo blinking lights page covers more Vertuo detail.

Vertuo and VertuoPlus (2018 manual)

This manual has the most complete red section we found. The manual lists red, green and orange light patterns.

Red patternWhat Nespresso says it meansWhat the manual says to do
Blinks twice, then steady greenWater tank empty, or capsule missing.Open the head to eject the capsule. Check a fresh capsule is inserted in the correct position and the head is closed, then press the button.
Blinks continuously and the head stops movingError, with the head stuck.Remove any obstacle, empty the capsule container if necessary, then open or close the head, depending on its position. Fill the tank and press the button.
Three blinks every 2 seconds, then steady redHead in an unknown position.Press the lever up, wait until the head stops, release any obstacle, empty the capsule container if necessary, then press the lever down to close the head.
Steady red, machine not runningDevice error.Turn off, unplug, wait 15 minutes for the machine to cool, plug in and turn on by pressing the lever down. It heats up and goes to steady green when ready. If the problem persists, call Nespresso.
Red fading out and back onCooling down after overheating.Nespresso says it goes to steady green when ready.
Half red, half green, steadyDescaling alert, not a fault. The manual says the machine can be used only a few more times before descaling is necessary.Descale. See Vertuo descaling.
Red flash twice, then back to blinking orangeError while a special function is running.Open the head to eject the capsule, then restart the function by pressing the button. If you were in the Emptying the system mode, remove the water tank.
Red flash twice, then steady orangeError in a special function.In the special functions menu, choose the function you want by pressing the lever down.

Vertuo Creatista

This is from the US BVE850 manual. The Creatista button lights use white, orange and red.

Red patternWhat the manual saysWhat the manual says to do
Coffee or steam button blinks red and the machine has stoppedThe listed check is that the water tank is filled.Fill the tank and press the coffee or steam button to restart. The manual also says to descale if necessary.
Coffee button shows two red blinks, then a pauseTroubleshooting table, for regular brewing: check a fresh undamaged capsule is in position, the handle is locked and the tank is filled. During descaling, emptying or cleaning: check the capsule is ejected and the handle is locked.If it persists: open the head, replace the capsule if needed, unplug for 10 seconds, plug in, close the head and press the button. If it still persists, call Nespresso.
Red light blinks rapidly twice, then long offListed as Error in the blinking summary, without further detail.We found no specific fix for this pattern.
Middle temperature light blinks red during descaling; bottom temperature light flashes red during the rinseDescribed in Nespresso's US Creatista descaling guide as part of the cycle.Nothing: the guide describes it as normal.

The same blinking summary lists an orange light that pulses down and then back on as cooling down after overheating. That one is orange, not red.

Vertuo Next and Vertuo Pop

Nespresso's Next FAQ (Australian site) is titled about blinking orange or red. It lists these common causes: machine not closed, empty tank, previous capsule not ejected, a possibly blocked upper part of the brew unit, capsule not recognised, electronic error and descaling needed. It does not say which is orange and which is red. Both manuals send you to a video for the full blinking summary.

Their written troubleshooting tables use blink counts instead of colours. For example, the Pop manual lists one blink and a pause as a tank or lever problem, and two blinks and a pause as a capsule, lever or tank problem. We do not add colours to those counts.

  • Empty tank: refill it.
  • Blocked brew unit: the FAQ says the upper part that punctures the capsule may be blocked. We found no steps for clearing it.
  • Capsule not recognised: Nespresso says to run the cleaning procedure twice.
  • Electronic error: disconnect the power cord and plug it back in after 10 seconds. If the problem persists, the FAQ says to do a factory reset.
  • Descale needed: the FAQ says every 3 months. See how to descale a Vertuo.

The Next manual says to call Nespresso if a problem persists. The FAQ also recommends the Nespresso app, which it says sends notifications when an issue occurs.

Original machines

On Pixie, Nespresso's Bahrain support page says a red backlight means the water tank is empty, and to refill it with potable water. We found no other red meaning for it. For the Essenza Mini and Inissia, we found no red light meaning. The Original patterns that are documented are blinks on the cup buttons, covered on our light meanings page.

Not found

  • A red light table in the newer VertuoPlus manual (the version we read has no red section), and any official red light meaning for Vertuo Lattissima.
  • Colour-to-alert mapping for the Next and Pop.
  • Red meanings for Original Lattissima, CitiZ&Milk or Creatista Plus.

Safety: unplug the machine before opening or cleaning it, and let it cool. A fault that returns after the steps above needs Nespresso support or a qualified technician.

Questions

Why is my Nespresso blinking red?

It depends on the family. On older VertuoPlus-style machines, Nespresso lists several red patterns: an empty tank or missing capsule, a blocked or mispositioned head, a device error and cooling down. On the Vertuo Creatista, a red blinking button after the machine stops points to the water tank. Nespresso's Bahrain Pixie support page says a red backlight means the tank is empty. Match your pattern in the tables above and check your own manual.

What does a solid red light mean on a Nespresso Vertuo?

In Nespresso's 2018 Vertuo and VertuoPlus manual, a steady red light with the machine not running is a device error. Nespresso says to switch it off, unplug it, wait about 15 minutes, then plug in and switch on. If it stays red, call Nespresso. Other families may differ, so check your manual.

Is a red and green light a fault on VertuoPlus?

No. Nespresso describes a button that is half red and half green as the descaling alert. Nespresso's 2018 manual says the machine can be used only a few more times after the alert before descaling becomes necessary. See our Vertuo descaling page.

Does a blinking red light on a Vertuo Next or Vertuo Pop have a published meaning?

Not one we could verify. Nespresso's Next FAQ is titled around blinking orange or red and lists common causes, but it does not match each cause to a colour or count. The Pop and Next manuals point to a video for their blinking summary. We do not give a colour table for those two models.

What about the Original Lattissima, Inissia or Essenza Mini?

We found no official red light meaning for them. Our Original machines page covers the blink patterns Nespresso does document.
