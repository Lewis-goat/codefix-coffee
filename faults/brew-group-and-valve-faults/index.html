What these parts do

On super-automatics the brew group (infuser) is a motor-driven chamber that compresses the grounds and forces water through. A ceramic or multi-way valve routes water between coffee, hot water and steam.

How they fail

The motor moved the group or valve but the position sensor never saw it arrive. Coffee oils, a jammed puck, scale stiffening a ceramic disc, or a failed encoder or motor all give a position error.

Safe checks first

  1. Switch off, empty the grounds container and drip tray, and restart. The startup cycle re-homes the group.
  2. Run the cleaning programme with the right tablet and let it finish uninterrupted.
  3. Descale if it is due. Scale is the usual cause of a stiff valve.
  4. If the brew unit is removable on your model, check for a jammed puck or broken plastic before forcing anything.

Appliances carry mains voltage. Unplug before opening anything, and stop where a code's own page tells you to.

Questions

How many codes involve brew group and valves?

We cover 10 codes on brew group and valves across 4 brands (Jura, Philips / Saeco, De'Longhi, Nespresso).

Can I fix these myself?

By our ratings, 7 are easy, 1 moderate and 2 hard. The cheap checks above clear a share of the easy and moderate ones; the hard ones usually need a part replaced.

Are any of these dangerous?

1 of the 10 are rated high severity, mostly where a heater or mains-powered part is involved. Stop using the machine and follow the stop advice on the code's page.
