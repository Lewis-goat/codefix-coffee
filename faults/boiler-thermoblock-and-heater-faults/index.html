What these parts do

Boilers and thermoblocks heat the water, and ovens and dryers use heating elements. A thermal fuse or thermostat cuts power if something overheats.

How they fail

The heater was switched on and the temperature did not climb, or it climbed too far. A blown thermal fuse, a failed element, scale insulating the heater, or a board relay stuck on can all be behind it.

Safe checks first

  1. Unplug and let the machine cool. Never test a heater that is hot or powered.
  2. Warm the machine to room temperature and restart once.
  3. Descale if due. Heavy scale slows heating enough to trip the check on some firmware.
  4. A heater that will not switch off is a safety issue. Do not leave the machine powered unattended.

Appliances carry mains voltage. Unplug before opening anything, and stop where a code's own page tells you to.

Questions

How many codes involve boilers and heaters?

We cover 11 codes on boilers and heaters across 4 brands (Breville / Sage, Jura, GE, Samsung).

Can I fix these myself?

By our ratings, 0 are easy, 3 moderate and 8 hard. The cheap checks above clear a share of the easy and moderate ones; the hard ones usually need a part replaced.

Are any of these dangerous?

7 of the 11 are rated high severity, mostly where a heater or mains-powered part is involved. Stop using the machine and follow the stop advice on the code's page.
