Breville Barista Express Impress troubleshooting: lights, alerts and fixes

What the tamp, dose, filter and maintenance lights mean on the Breville Barista Express Impress (BES876), what its alerts are, and how Breville says to clean and descale it.

Scope: the Impress with the assisted tamp lever and the dose level gauge. The older push-button Barista Express is covered on our Barista Express page. Breville does not publish numbered error codes for this machine in the books we read.

Lights and alerts

What you seeWhat Breville says it meansWhat to do
POWER button flashing at startThe machine is heating. The ANZ book says up to about 40 seconds.Wait. The 1 CUP, 2 CUP and POWER lights come on when it is ready.
DOSE button not litThe portafilter is not seated, the hopper is not locked, or the tamp lever is not at home.Fix each in turn.
DOSE button blinking during grindingThe light blinks until the grind cycle is completed.Wait.
TAMP light blinking after grindingThe grind is done and it is time to tamp.Push the lever down until the beep, then release slowly.
TAMP light keeps flashing, no reading on the dose gaugeThe tamp was not completed.Push down until you hear the beep.
DOSE button flashing after a tampUnder dose, "A Bit More" mode.Press DOSE to add grounds, tamp again.
Dose bar above the middleOver dose.Take the portafilter out and trim with the Razor trimming tool. The machine works out how much to cut from the next auto grind.
Filter lights alternating single and doubleNo hopper warning: hopper not attached correctly.Seat and lock the hopper.
DOSE button and dose lights flash red, three beepsLow coffee beans warning.Add beans.
Three beeps and the dose cycle pausesThe tamp lever is not at the home position.Raise the lever, then press DOSE to carry on.
Maintenance light flashingA cleaning cycle is required.See cleaning cycle below.
Maintenance light solidDescaling is required.See descaling below.

Dose and tamp problems

SymptomCause Breville listsFix
Lever very hard to pushSevere over dose, or coffee build-up in the mechanism.Trim the dose with the Razor and choose the right filter size. Take off the tamp cover and brush the mechanism clean.
Puck surface rough or has loose groundsGrounds on the tamp face, or tamping too fast.Clean the tamp face. Push the lever down more slowly, or tamp a second time.
Tamp height not what you wantAuto mode only aims at the ideal height.Use manual mode.
Auto time not adjusting or dose level not updatingThe machine is in manual mode, or in top-up mode.Press the manual dose dial to go back to auto. In top-up, do not remove the portafilter before grinding ends.
Severe over doseSingle basket used with double filter setting.Select the right filter size.
Severe under doseDouble basket used with single setting.Select the right size, then reset the auto dose time.
Under dose after changing the grindGrind set finer.Use "A Bit More".
Grinder does not use all the beansNot enough beans in the hopper.Refill, or use manual mode.

Breville says changing the grind size is reflected in the auto dose only after your next tamp. If you change the grind a lot, or dosed twice by accident, it suggests resetting the auto dose time.

Coffee, steam and other faults

SymptomCause Breville listsFix
No cremaStale beans.Breville says to use beans with a Roasted On date and use them between 5 and 20 days after it.
No coffee from the portafilterGrind too fine, or empty tank.Grind coarser, refill the tank.
Coffee runs too fast or too slowGrind too coarse (fast) or too fine (slow).Adjust the grind size.
Pressure gauge not workingWrong dose or too little coffee in the basket, or beans beyond their expiry date.Use dual wall baskets or a finer grind setting. Use freshly roasted beans within the 5 to 20 day window. Call Breville Consumer Support if it persists.
No water from the group headNot at temperature, tank empty or not locked in.Wait, refill, push the tank in until the latch closes.
No steamSteam wand blocked, or empty tank.Use the pin on the cleaning tool. If it stays blocked, see Cleaning the Steam Wand in the manual.
Water spits from the wandThe machine is still heating.Purge the wand before frothing milk.
Hot water stops flowingMachine too hot after steaming.Let it cool.
Machine on but does nothingMachine is too hot.Switch off, wait 30 to 60 minutes, switch back on. If it persists, call Breville Consumer Support.

Cleaning, descaling and resets

Cleaning cycle (flashing maintenance light)

This is a back-flush and is not descaling. The AU/NZ book steps: put the 1 CUP basket in the portafilter, then the cleaning disc, then one tablet. Lock the portafilter in and put a large container below. Have a full cold tank and an empty drip tray. Switch the machine off, then hold 1 CUP, 2 CUP and POWER for 3 seconds. The cycle takes about 5 minutes. Check the tablet has dissolved and repeat if not. Then remove the disc, put the portafilter back and press 2 CUP to rinse the brew head.

Descaling (solid maintenance light)

Take the water filter out of the tank first. Breville says not to use bottled water when descaling, because the dissolved solids can leave deposits inside. Dissolve one descaling sachet fully in 1 litre of water and fill the tank. With the machine off, hold 2 CUP and POWER for 5 seconds. The machine heats, and is ready when POWER, 2 CUP, the CLEAN / DESCALE light and the hot water / steam light are solid. Press 2 CUP for the coffee stage (about 25 seconds). Turn the steam dial right for steam (about 13 seconds) and left for hot water (about 8 seconds). About half a litre should be left. Repeat to empty the tank, then remove the tank, rinse it, refill with fresh cold water to the maximum mark and repeat to rinse the machine. Press POWER to exit. After 5 minutes of no input the machine switches off and you restart from the entry step.

The ANZ book says to change the water filter every three months, and sooner in hard water areas. The figures here come from the BES876 book and should not be applied to other models.

Resets

  • Reset auto dose time: in standby, hold MANUAL DOSE and FILTER for 3 seconds. The lights flash three times with beeps.
  • Reset extraction time: in standby, hold FILTER, 1 CUP and 2 CUP for 3 seconds. Three beeps, then ready mode.

These steps are from the AU/NZ and UK editions we read. Check the book that came with your machine, since button names can differ by market.

Safety: unplug the machine before cleaning, and keep the cord and plug away from water. The group head, steam wand and hot water outlet are hot. If a fault persists, contact Breville Consumer Support.

Things we could not verify

  • Numbered error codes. We found none in Breville's books.
  • The US edition. The tables above come from the AU/NZ book. The UK and US editions were only spot-checked, so wording and button names may differ in yours.
  • Internal repairs. We give no pump or internal repair advice, because we found no official source for it.

Questions

Why is the tamp light still flashing after I tamped?

Breville says the tamp was not finished. Push the lever all the way down until you hear the beep, then bring it back up slowly. The tamp light flashes by design once grinding is done, to show it is ready for you to tamp.

Why is the DOSE button flashing?

In auto mode it means the dose came out under the ideal bar and the machine is offering "A Bit More". Press DOSE to add a little, then tamp again to check.

Why do the single and double filter lights flash back and forth?

Breville calls this the no hopper warning. The bean hopper is not locked in place. Check it is seated and turn it to lock.

What does it mean when the maintenance light flashes or stays on?

Flashing means a cleaning cycle is due. Solid means descaling is due. They are separate procedures in the Care and Cleaning section of the manual.

Why will the machine not grind?

Breville lists an empty hopper, a hopper not locked, a portafilter not fully in the cradle, a tamp lever left too low so the tamp blocks the chute, and coffee build-up in the cradle. The DOSE button only lights when the portafilter, hopper and lever are all in the right place.

Is the Barista Express Impress the same as the Barista Express?

No. This page covers the BES876 with the assisted tamp lever and dose level gauge. The older push-button model has a different panel, covered on our Barista Express page.
