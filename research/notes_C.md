# SERP notes — candidates C1–C9 (searched 2026-09-02)

Method: 3–5 seed queries per niche via web search; noted who ranks in the top ~8, weak signals (forum/Quora/FB/old/thin/off-intent), calculator-farm presence (omnicalculator / calculator.net / inchcalculator / calculatorsoup), and whether a one-line AI answer closes the query.

## C1 — Ingredient-specific cooking converters (23/40)
- `cups to grams flour`: inchcalculator #1, then christinesrecipes (2011), eatlittlebird, thecalculatorsite x2, gramstocup.com, and two spam pages on stripe.jhu.edu. Old + spam = weak signals, but the intent is closed by an AI one-liner.
- `cups to grams brown sugar`: bakeprofit, howmany.wiki, coolconversion, flourwise, thebakingcalculator, traditionaloven, cookingconverter, bakingscalepro, Scribd PDF. Eight programmatic clones already.
- `tablespoons to grams butter`: inchcalculator, omnicalculator, bakinglikeachef, calculateme x2, cookconvertapp, traditionaloven, errenskitchen.
- `ml to grams honey`: thecalculatorsite, chefsolver, ezunitconverter x2, mltograms.com, coolconversion, howmany.wiki, traditionaloven x2.
- `cup to gram converter oats`: chefsolver, gramstocup.org, bakeprofit, cups2grams, cookconvertapp, conversionencyclopedia, unitzzz, cookingconverter.
- Calculator farms: inchcalculator/omni/thecalculatorsite present on 3 of 5 but do NOT own the SERP — the small clones do.
- Verdict: small sites clearly rank, but the niche is saturated by identical programmatic sites, AI Overview answers every query, and food CPC is ~$0.10–0.30. Best gap: none worth the effort; maybe "packed vs spooned" per-ingredient density tables, already done by bakingscalepro.

## C2 — Strength-training calculators (26/40)
- `1rm calculator`: builtwithscience, barbellmedicine, NASM, strengthlevel, App Store, ironbullstrength, calculator.net, kingofweighted, App Store. Authority-heavy.
- `plate calculator barbell`: strengthlevel, Google Play, App Store x2, exrx.net, legend-tracker, gympsycho, barisloadedapp, carlosrendon.me (personal page = weak).
- `rpe chart`: triathlete.com, two bioRxiv/USPTO PDFs on retinal pigment epithelium (term collision), Cleveland Clinic, reactivetrainingsystems, NASM blog, a Squarespace PDF. Mixed/polluted SERP = weak, but the query is a static table AI can render.
- `weekly volume calculator hypertrophy`: rpstrength (authority), then 9 tiny tool sites (strengthtrainingtools, rpetraining, gymvalid, tracked.gg, top10k, arvo.guru, bitekit, trainerstudio, betterlifefitness). Clearly open to small sites — but already 9 of them.
- `wilks calculator`: strengthlevel, omni, fitnessvolt, Wikipedia, Powerlifting Australia, weighttraining.nz, legend-tracker, wilkscalculator.com, liftercalc.com.
- Farms: omni + calculator.net on head terms.
- YMYL: calculators themselves are fine; any "how much should you train / injury" text drifts into health advice. Keep it math-only.
- Best gap: RPE x reps -> %1RM lookup with per-lift adjustments, and MEV/MAV/MRV per-muscle planners — the long tail (per-exercise volume-landmark pages, e.g. "lateral raise weekly sets") is where small sites rank. Personal edge (hypertrophy) is the strongest of any C candidate.

Best gap: expansion-specific edge cases (e.g. Catan Seafarers pirate movement, Wingspan Oceania nectar) — real but tiny volume.

## C4 — Appliance error-code lookup by brand (28/40)
- `samsung washer error code 4c`: Samsung UK support, iFixit guide, Samsung Community, YouTube x2, Facebook group posts x3, JustAnswer, Samsung Community again. Three FB posts + JustAnswer in top 10 = very weak below the brand page.
- `lg dishwasher error code oe`: LG support x2, hoffmannbros (regional HVAC/plumber blog), iFixit answers, northlondonappliancerepairs forum, JustAnswer x2.
- `bosch dishwasher e24`: iFixit x2, Bosch official, PartSelect, Quora, beaconsaves, JustAnswer, atoz1 (local repair).
- `whirlpool oven error code f2`: PartSelect, Whirlpool product help x2, Facebook x2, JustAnswer x2, doityourself.com forum, Walmart, eBay.de junk.
- `ge dryer error code e8`: JustAnswer x3, TikTok, samedayappliancerepair, easybear-appliancerepair x2. No GE official page in results — clear hole.
- Calculator farms: none (not a calculator niche); the "farm" equivalents are JustAnswer and local repair-shop SEO blogs, which are far weaker.
- AI Overview: gives the generic cause (drain/inlet) but not model-specific reset sequences, part numbers, or multi-meaning codes (GE E8 has 3 meanings) — structured per-model tables still add value.
- Ad value: appliance parts / repair CPC is high ($2–5); PartSelect/RepairClinic affiliates exist.
- Best gap: brand x appliance x code matrix (est. 40 brands x 4 appliance types x 20–60 codes), with a "which meaning applies to your model" disambiguator, especially for GE/Frigidaire/Maytag where the brand's own page is missing or buried. Risk: needs accurate source data (service manuals) — wrong info is user-harmful and reputationally costly; also easy for AI to summarise the common cases.

## Cross-cutting
- Calculator-farm ownership: C8 (total), C1 (partial, but cloned by dozens), C7 mulch/spacing, C5 chain/tire. Farms are largely absent from C3, C4, C6, and most of C9/C2-longtail.
- Weakest SERPs by authority: C6 > C3 > C4 (below brand page) > C9 > C5.
- Most AI-Overview-proof: C8, C5, C6, C9 (need interactive/table); least: C1, C3.
- Rank order by total: C4 28; C2/C5/C7/C9 26; C6/C8 25; C1 23; C3 20.
- For a ~$1.25/mo target, C4 and C7 have the volume x CPC headroom; C2 and C9 suit the owner's skills better (C2 has the deepest personal edge, C9 leans on optics/software).
