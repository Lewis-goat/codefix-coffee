Jura error code questions

How many Jura error codes are covered?

16 codes across 1 machine family. Each has its own page with the meaning, the part at fault, the fix and the cost.

What does Jura Error 2 mean?

Coffee temperature sensor interrupted, or the machine is too cold to heat. Error 2 is the most common Jura code and it has a benign cause and a real one. Benign: the machine is below about 10 C (delivered in winter, kept in a cold room) and the heater is locked out until it warms. Real: the coffee thermoblock sensor or the thermal-fuse cords have gone open circuit.

What does Jura Error 7 mean?

Ceramic valve / multi-valve or brew-group drive fault (valve did not reach its commanded position). Error 7 is the valve-side sibling of Error 8: the machine commanded the ceramic valve (on GIGA models the multi-valve) to a position and never saw it arrive, or the brew-group drive misbehaved on the way. Practitioner tables describe it as a ceramic valve, encoder, or brew-group malfunction; on GIGA X3c/X8c it specifically indicates a faulty multi-valve, and on the GIGA 6 it is often reported as a motor or pump blockage. It is one of the few Jura codes with no reliable user-level fix.

What does Jura Error 31 mean?

We could not find a verified meaning. Jura's own manuals define only Errors 2 and 5, and the sources that claim a meaning for 31 conflict or cite nothing. Jura's instruction for any error other than 2 or 5 is to switch the machine off with the On/Off button and contact customer support.

Why do Jura error numbers mean different things on different machines?

Technician service tables number the GIGA line differently from the standard E, ENA, S, J and Z machines. For example Error 13 is the right grinder on a GIGA and the left on a standard machine. Each Jura page says which family it describes.

Can I fix Jura errors myself?

By our ratings, 2 of the 16 codes are easy, 6 moderate and 8 hard or not recommended for DIY. Each page lists the cheapest checks first and says where to stop.
