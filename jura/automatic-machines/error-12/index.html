Error 12Jura

Jura Error 12: grinder jam or motor fault

Error 12 means: Grinder failed to activate — the motor is seized or the burrs are impeded, most often by a stone picked up with the beans (GIGA: left grinder).

Applies to: automatic machines (E6, E8, ENA, S8, J8, Z10, GIGA).

Short answerThe control board commanded a grind and the grinder never turned. On two-grinder GIGA machines Error 12 names the left grinder. In practice it is nearly always a foreign object — classically a small stone from a bean harvest — jamming the burrs, or a motor that has seized on old, oily fines. On the GIGA 6 the same number can instead flag a communication or sensor fault, which a power cycle addresses first.
Part at faultgrinder motor and burrs

How to fix Error 12

  1. Unplug for five minutes, then restart — on GIGA 6 this clears the communication-fault variant.
  2. Empty the bean hopper completely and inspect the beans at the bottom for stones or clumped roasts.
  3. Vacuum the hopper outlet and the grinder throat with a narrow nozzle; do not poke metal into the burrs.
  4. Empty the grounds container and drip tray, reinsert both, and restart so the machine re-homes.
  5. If the grinder hums without turning once clear, the motor is seized and needs replacement.

When to stop and call a repairer

Burr and grinder-motor replacement on a GIGA means significant teardown — a bench repair unless you already service these.

What it costs

Can I fix Error 12 myself?

How much does it cost to fix Error 12?

Grinder motor $60 to $140; burr set $25 to $45. Out-of-warranty manufacturer service for a super-automatic is typically $250 to $500 including return shipping; independent espresso repairers are usually cheaper for a single part.

Is it worth repairing?

Yes on GIGA and Z machines — check your beans for stones before refilling.

When does Error 12 typically show up?

Juras report Error 12 any. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with Error 12 showing?

Usually you can finish the drink you are making, but do not ignore it — the underlying fault rarely heals itself. Work through the cheap checks first.

Will Error 12 clear on its own?

Sometimes. Try the reset and cleaning steps first — they cost nothing and clear the milder cases. A code that returns within a day or two needs the parts fix.

How long does the repair take?

Thirty minutes to an hour if you are comfortable opening the case; budget an afternoon the first time.
