Jura Fill water tank (tank is full): false "fill water tank" message

Fill water tank (tank is full) means: Water tank message will not clear although the tank is full.

Short answerThe machine reads the water level through a magnet float in the tank and a sensor in the machine. Scale on the float, a float stuck at the bottom, or a tank not seated on its valve gives a false empty reading.
Part at faulttank float / level sensor
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix Fill water tank (tank is full)

  1. Remove the tank, check the float in its channel moves freely, and descale the tank in a bowl of descaler.
  2. Reseat the tank firmly; the valve at the base must open.
  3. Fit a fresh filter correctly, or remove it, and try again.
  4. If the message persists with a clean float and full tank, the reed sensor in the machine has failed.

When to stop and call a repairer

Nothing dangerous here.
