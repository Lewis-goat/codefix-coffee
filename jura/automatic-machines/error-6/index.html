Jura Error 6: ceramic valve fault

Error 6 means: Electronic ceramic valve not operating correctly.

Short answer. Machines with the electronic ceramic valve (Z5 to Z10, X series, J5 to J9, GIGA, newer S and E) route water between coffee, hot water and steam with a motor-driven ceramic disc. Error 6 means the disc did not reach the position the board asked for: scale has stiffened it, a leak has got into the drive, or the motor or its position sensor has failed.
Part at faultceramic valve and its drive motor
Severitymedium
DIY difficultyhard
When it appearsany

How to fix Error 6

  1. Run a full descale first; scale is the number-one cause and the fix is free.
  2. Restart. If the valve now cycles (you will hear it click through positions on startup) you are done.
  3. If it persists, open the machine and check for water around the valve body; a leaking valve seal wets the motor and encoder.
  4. Clean or replace the valve; the motor and valve are usually replaced as an assembly.

When to stop and call a repairer

Valve assemblies are fiddly and the machine must be fully drained first; this is a common professional repair.

What it costs

Parts: Ceramic valve assembly $60 to $120; seal kit $10 to $20.

Service: Out-of-warranty manufacturer service for a super-automatic is typically $250 to $500 including return shipping; independent espresso repairers are usually cheaper for a single part.

Worth repairing? Yes, the machines that have this valve are worth keeping.
