Jura Error 5: heater not reaching temperature

Error 5 means: Heating failure: the machine cannot reach temperature.

Short answer. The heater was switched on and the temperature did not climb. On a Jura that is almost always the thermal-fuse cords that protect the thermoblock, which blow after an overheat or with age; a dead thermoblock element is the other cause.
Part at faultthermal fuse cords / thermoblock
Severityhigh
DIY difficultyhard
When it appearsany

How to fix Error 5

  1. Warm the machine to room temperature (a very cold machine also triggers this) and restart.
  2. Inside, meter the two thermal-fuse cords on the affected thermoblock. Open circuit means replace them.
  3. Meter the thermoblock element. Open circuit means a new thermoblock.
  4. Find out why the fuses blew: scale, a stuck relay on the power board, or a dry-fire when the tank ran empty.

When to stop and call a repairer

Mains voltage on the thermoblock terminals.

What it costs

Parts: Fuse cord set $15 to $30; thermoblock $90 to $180.

Service: Out-of-warranty manufacturer service for a super-automatic is typically $250 to $500 including return shipping; independent espresso repairers are usually cheaper for a single part.

Worth repairing? Yes for fuse cords. Thermoblock plus board on an old machine: compare with a refurbished unit.
