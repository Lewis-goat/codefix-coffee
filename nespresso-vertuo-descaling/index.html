How to descale a Nespresso Vertuo machine

A summary of Nespresso's descaling procedures for Vertuo Next, Pop, VertuoPlus and Creatista, with what the light does at each stage. Amounts and button holds differ by model and region, so follow the guide that came with your machine.

What you need: Nespresso's own descaling kit and an empty container under the outlet. Nespresso's US VertuoPlus guide says other solutions, such as vinegar or store-bought descalers, may cause damage. Expect about 20 minutes (25 for the Creatista guide). Our red light page and Vertuo blinking lights page cover other lights.

Safety: the solution can irritate eyes and skin. Keep it from children. The US guide says it can damage or discolor surfaces, so wipe spills at once. The manuals say never to put the machine in a dishwasher or immerse it in water.

Vertuo Next and Pop

The Pop and Next manuals give the same descaling text, apart from wording differences noted below. The Next support page adds details.

  1. With the button blinking orange 3 times, unlock and open the head so the used capsule ejects. Empty the capsule container and drip tray and put them back.
  2. Put 0.5 L of water and one unit of descaling liquid in the tank.
  3. Remove the cup support and place an empty container of at least 1 L under the outlet.
  4. With the machine on, close the head and leave the lever unlocked.
  5. Hold the button for at least 7 seconds, or until the light turns off. It then blinks orange rapidly. The manuals say you have 45 seconds to enter descaling mode, or the machine returns to brewing.
  6. Lock the head by turning the lever left, then unlock it by turning right. Hold the button again for about 7 seconds, or until the light turns off. It blinks orange rapidly.
  7. Lock the machine by turning the lever left. If the light is still blinking orange quickly, you are in descaling mode.
  8. Press the coffee button once. The light blinks orange throughout, and the machine stops automatically when the first phase ends.
  9. Rinse the tank and drip tray, refill the tank with fresh water, empty the container and put it back.
  10. Press the button to rinse. The Pop manual says descaling is complete when the button stops blinking rapidly. The Next manual adds that it becomes steady.
  11. Wash the capsule container, cup support and drip tray with hot water and mild odourless detergent. Dry the machine with a clean towel and let it dry for 10 minutes before use.

The Next and Pop manuals say descaling cannot be interrupted. The Next support page says that if you unplug during descaling, it restarts in descaling mode and you must repeat descaling and rinsing.

VertuoPlus

  1. When the button is half red and half green, switch on, open the head so the capsule ejects, and empty the container.
  2. Close the head and hold the lever down 3 seconds to switch off.
  3. Fill the tank with 0.5 L (17 fl oz) of water and one unit of descaling liquid (the US guide says at least 17 oz of water). Remove the cup support and put an empty container under the outlet: the manual says at least 1 L, the US guide at least 27 oz.
  4. With the machine off, push the lever down and press the button for 3 seconds. A steady orange light means the special functions menu.
  5. Push the lever down once for descaling mode, then press the button once. Orange blinks throughout the cycle.
  6. When it stops, rinse the tank and drip tray, refill with fresh water, empty the container, then press the button once to rinse. The manual says orange blinks during rinsing and a steady green light means rinsing is done.
  7. To exit descaling mode, push the lever down and press the button together for 3 seconds. Wash the capsule container and cup support, then let the machine dry 10 minutes (the US guide says to let it rest 10 minutes).

The US guide says you can stop the process at any time with the button. The manual says descaling cannot be interrupted, but also says the rinse can be stopped by pressing the button. We cannot reconcile the two.

Vertuo Creatista

This model has a steam wand, so the steps differ. The US guide says to clean the steam wand tip first. Only the main points are given here.

  1. Eject any capsule, empty the capsule container and drip tray, and put them back. Fill the tank with 33.81 oz of water to the marked descaling line and add 2 packets of descaling solution. Put an empty container of at least 65 oz under the outlet and steam wand. With the machine on, close the head and lock the lever.
  2. Press the Steam and Milk Temperature buttons for about 5 seconds until the descaling light is steady yellow, the bottom and middle temperature lights are on and the steam button flashes orange.
  3. Press and release Steam to start. The guide says the middle temperature light blinks red during descaling.
  4. When the cycle ends, the Steam button flashes and only the bottom temperature light is on. Discard the solution, rinse the tank, capsule container and drip tray, refill the tank to MAX with fresh water, put the container back and press Steam to rinse.
  5. The bottom temperature light flashes red during the rinse. When rinsing ends, the machine leaves descaling by itself and the lights return to ready. Empty and rinse the parts, refill the tank, wipe the machine with a damp cloth and let it rest 10 minutes.

The US guide says the cycle can be paused with the Steam button and resumed by pressing it again. The BVE850 manual describes a similar procedure with 2 units of solution, filling to the descale line and a container of at least 1.5 L. Check which applies to your version.

If descaling does not finish

  • The Next and Pop manuals say to check that no capsule is inserted, then close and lock the machine before starting.
  • The US VertuoPlus guide says to avoid filling the water tank during the descaling process.
  • On the Next, the support page says that after power is cut, both the descaling and rinsing cycles must be repeated.
  • If it will not start, remember the 30 coffee minimum on Next, Pop and VertuoPlus.
  • If a light still blinks and you do not mean to descale, the Creatista manual says to press the button to stop, then hold it for at least 7 seconds to exit descaling mode. We found no equivalent for the Pop.

Questions

How often should I descale a Nespresso Vertuo?

Nespresso's US VertuoPlus guide says every 3 months or 300 capsules, whichever comes first. The Vertuo Next FAQ says every 3 months. The Pop and Creatista documents we read give no fixed interval and rely on the machine alert. Check your own manual.

Can I use vinegar?

Nespresso says to use its own descaling kit. Its VertuoPlus guide warns that other solutions, including vinegar, may damage the machine.

Why will my Vertuo not enter descaling mode?

Nespresso manuals for the Next, Pop and VertuoPlus say the machine can only enter descaling mode after 30 or more coffees. On the Next and Pop you also have 45 seconds to finish the entry steps, or it returns to brewing. Make sure the head is closed, no capsule is inside, and follow the order for your model.

Can I stop descaling halfway?

Sources differ. The Next and Pop manuals say the procedure cannot be interrupted, and a Next support page says unplugging mid-process restarts descaling and rinsing. Nespresso's VertuoPlus and Creatista guides describe a way to stop or pause. Follow the guide for your model.

What if the light keeps blinking after I finish?

Check that you ran the rinse cycle as well as the descale cycle. Nespresso says the Next light goes steady, and the VertuoPlus light goes steady green, when rinsing is done. On the VertuoPlus, the manual says to exit descaling mode by pushing the lever down and pressing the button together for 3 seconds. The Creatista manual says to hold the button for at least 7 seconds. If it persists, contact Nespresso.
